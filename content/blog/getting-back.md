---
title: "Getting back"
date: "2026-11-07"
---

I've been dealing with a lot of issues lately - my health, my career, my finances, and life in general. But I'm much more resilient now.

I had a nasty motorcycle accident last year which led to a full ACL and PCL reconstruction and a full meniscus repair. Recovery was painful and took a lot of time.

And when I got back on my feet, I realized I need to quit my job. I guess I didn't like being mediocre.

Right then, I had a migraine aura attack so intense it landed me in the hospital. It was misdiagnosed as a stroke. It took a couple more attacks to get the right diagnosis.

More and more issues kept popping up. But the recent one was devastating. I slipped on a wet floor and fell directly on the bad knee.

I'm back to square one - hell. I'm in pain and struggling to focus. Heck, I can't even sit and study for long. And I might be looking at another surgery.

All these physical issues really did a number on my mental health too. But nothing can stop me from wanting to get back, despite all these setbacks. I just wanna be back, just deeply entangled in systems.

I know life ain't supposed to be smooth. From the beginning, I knew my life would never be comfortable and I'd never be at what people call "peace".

So, I simply embraced the chaos. Finding the beauty of life in it.
