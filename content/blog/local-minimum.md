---
title: Local Minimum
date: 21-08-2026
---

I recently swallowed a bitter pill: I suck

No kidding. I have no talent, no intellect, and no skills.

I deluded myself into believing that I could work towards becoming a decent person. Now, I'm no longer inebriated by own delusion. I see how things work and where I stand.

I lack the fundamentals. I'm not smart. And I lack the discipline.

Heck I'm not even good at life. I lack serious life skills.

For most of my life, I pretended that I knew a decent amount of stuff. The lesson I learnt the hard way was: stop pretending.

Almost every idea I had failed badly. Most of the ideas themselves sucked too.

I haven't achieved anything. I haven't contributed anything meaningful to the engineering community. Not a single notable thing I've done in my life that I can show off with pride.

These are not thoughts of early-career anxiety or a mid-life crisis. These feel like hard, verifiable facts.

I'd constantly make mistakes. I don't think I have ever slept a night without regret. My heart feels heavy most of the time.

I am now at the lowest point of my life.

Of course, I can't make that prediction with certainty. Maybe this is only a local minimum in a two-dimensional space, and something much lower is ahead.

But so far, yes, this is the lowest.

Still, thousands of years of evolution inside me tells me I need to get back up. To tidy myself and keep moving. This is not over yet.

But I lack the energy to do so, I feel weaker. But I need to get back up. One constant thought. From somewhere deep inside me.

I know how.

I know why.

The question is: will I?
